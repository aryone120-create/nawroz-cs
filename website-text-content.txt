─────────────────────────────
  FAQ SECTION  (id="faq")
─────────────────────────────
Section label: "// BEFORE YOU DECIDE"
H2: "Questions, answered."

Q1: "Do I need coding experience to start?"
A1: "None at all. We start from zero. What we look for is curiosity and the willingness to build — the rest, we teach you, step by step."

Q2: "What language are courses taught in?"
A2: "Courses are taught in Kurdish and English. Programming is naturally an English-literate craft, and we build that skill with you as you go — a lasting advantage in itself."

Q3: "How is this different from the medical programs?"
A3: "Different worlds entirely. CS is about creating and inventing rather than memorizing. It is a four-year Bachelor's degree, and your work is not tied to a single place or profession."

Q4: "Who teaches the courses?"
A4: "Experienced professionals who have built real software and systems — people who bring the practice of the field, not only its theory, into the room."

Q5: "What can I actually build here?"
A5: "AI models, video games, robots, websites, mobile apps, and desktop software. Six worlds, one department — you choose which to master."
